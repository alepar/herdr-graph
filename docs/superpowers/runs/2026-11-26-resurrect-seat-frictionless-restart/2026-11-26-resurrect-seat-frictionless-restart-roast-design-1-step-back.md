changes: The event-based gate for keyless relaunch after a Herdr restart: §4.1 "Keyless relaunches wait for `rebind` done in the same incarnation", with its release rule in §4.2 (only definitive refusals release it) and the emission rule in the §4 opening paragraph (emitted once, on an `Unresolved` fact read after the change).
to: §4 opening and §4.1-4.2 restated as level-triggered state rules. (a) Emission: while a seated clone's fact reads `Unresolved` in the current incarnation and its bound pane exists, the planner emits `threads.rebind_held_seat` (op key per incarnation; a transient or errored attempt is retried each pass until the grace ends; a definitive refusal is recorded once). A fact still reading `Resolved` on a pane whose incarnation changed counts as threads not caught up: nothing is emitted yet and the gate stays closed (same caught-up guard as closes, src/threads/effects.rs ~546). (b) Gate: a keyless relaunch waits until the seat fact reads `Resolved` on its own pane in this incarnation, or until the 90 s grace since the incarnation change has passed, then proceeds whatever the rebind outcome; keyed relaunches never wait. (c) Status: §4.5 fires when the grace ends with the fact still `Unresolved`, whatever happened to the rebind. §4.2 shrinks to how refusals are recorded. The keyless rows of the §4 table read "relaunch waits until the seat is placed or the grace expires".
