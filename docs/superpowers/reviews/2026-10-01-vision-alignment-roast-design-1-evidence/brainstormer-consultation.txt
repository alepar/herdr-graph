  Essential promises

  • Durable team/seat/clone identity; concurrent conversations within one responsibility; direct human relationships with specialists. See graph experience (HERDR-
    GRAPH-VISION.md:25) and current identity model (CURRENT-DESIGN.md:5).

  • Templates and relationships must produce maintained organizational structure, beyond labels and prompts. Professions, hierarchy, and behavioral policy remain

    recurring work. Transcript restoration alone does not restore scheduled jobs. See recovery example (HERDR-GRAPH-VISION.md:35) and factory recovery practice
    (SOFTWARE-FACTORY-VISION.md:77).

  • Peer learning remains an agent practice supported by shared references and threads—not automatic synchronization of model knowledge.
